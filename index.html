RenOS Frequently Asked Questions

What Is RenOS?

RenOS is an AI operating system for teams. It gives your people one workspace across many AI models, routes each task by your rules, explains every decision in plain language and writes an audit entry automatically. Read what an AI operating system is.

How Is It Different from a Single AI Assistant?

An assistant answers questions. RenOS sits across every assistant and model your team uses and adds the policy layer, audit trail and admin view across all of them.

Which AI Models Does It Work With?

RenOS reaches hundreds of models through one workspace, across text and reasoning, image, audio and video. See supported AI models.

Where Does Our Data Go?

You choose which models and suppliers each task may use, by data sensitivity, task type or role. Every choice is logged with its reason.

Can It Run in Our Own Environment?

Yes. Private or local deployment keeps data inside your environment.
