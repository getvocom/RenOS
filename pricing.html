Questions

Pricing Questions

Is There a Free Plan?

No. RenOS governs teams, so a solo free seat wouldn't show you what it does. Start with a pilot instead: its fee is credited to your first year.

What Happens to Unused Credits?

Pro's included credits pool across your team each month. Purchased credits stay valid for 12 months.

Where Do the Models Come From?

Through our carrier partner Vocom, or your own keys. Admins can restrict sensitive data to verified supply only. See RenOS Credits.

Do Admins and Auditors Need a Paid Seat?

No. Admin, auditor and read-only compliance seats are always free.

Can We Use Our Own Model Provider Contract?

Yes, on Enterprise. Bring your own keys and RenOS still applies policy and writes the audit trail.

Can a Partner Resell RenOS?

Yes. Carriers, MSPs and IT partners can resell RenOS to the organizations they support. See the partner program.
