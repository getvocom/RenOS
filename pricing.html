Questions

Pricing Questions

Is There a Free Plan?

No. RenOS governs teams, so a solo free seat wouldn't show you what it does. Start with a 60-day pilot instead.

What Happens to Unused Credits?

Pro's included credits pool across your team each month. Talk to us about purchased credits.

Where Do the Models Come From?

Through RenOS, or through your own keys on Enterprise. Admins can restrict sensitive data to approved models only. See supported AI models.

Do Admins and Auditors Need a Paid Seat?

No. Admin, auditor and read-only compliance seats are always free.

Can We Use Our Existing AI Contracts?

Yes, on Enterprise. Bring your own keys and RenOS still applies policy and writes the audit trail.

Can Partners Offer RenOS to Their Clients?

Yes. MSPs and IT partners can offer RenOS to the organizations they support. See the partner program.
