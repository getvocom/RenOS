Governance Questions

What Is a Governed Session?

One task from request to accepted result: who ran it, on which model, under which policy, with what outcome, written as the work happens.

Can a Record Be Changed After the Fact?

Each record is chained to the one before it. Any change breaks the chain and shows up on verification.

How Are New Rules Tested?

Every rule is linted and simulated against the last 30 days of sessions before it's published. Lint and simulation are deterministic; no model grades its own policy.

How Long Are Records Kept?

90 days on Pro, and a year or more with export on Enterprise.
