About

Why We Built RenOS

AI is spreading through organizations one subscription at a time, and almost nobody can prove what it did. We built RenOS to fix that.

The Problem We Kept Seeing

Every team we spoke with was already using AI. Sales had one tool, operations another, and a few people had their own. Data went into all of them. When someone asked what the AI had done with a client's information, nobody could answer.

Our Approach

We think governance should come first and stay invisible. People should be able to use any model that suits the task, while the organization decides the rules and gets a record it can trust. That's why RenOS puts policy routing before every model call and writes an AI audit trail automatically.

Where We Are

Design-partner pilots are open. Multi-agent identities, access to hundreds of models and audit logging have been demoed live, and our design partners help shape what comes next. Join the pilot.
