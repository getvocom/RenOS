Multi-Agent Crews

Patterns You Can Run, With Caps You Set

A crew is named roles, one objective and hard caps, and it runs as one governed session. Reviewers never grade their own work.

Planner, Then Executors

A planner makes the plan; executors build the units in parallel. Better control and observability for long workflows.

Visionary and Integrator

A second vendor re-runs the proof independently. Verdicts: Same page, Revise, Reject, or Escalate to you as referee.

Verifier-Gated Actions

An independent verifier checks rules before anything leaves the building. Tier-3 actions always wait for a human.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RenOS Another AI Assistant?

No. RenOS is the layer across the AI models your team uses: one workspace, one policy, one audit trail.

Do People Have to Learn Which Model to Use?

No. Auto is the default. They pick a mode and describe the task, and policy picks the model and explains why. Choosing a model by hand is a governed override.

Does It Run on Our Laptops?

RenOS runs on a server, so your whole team signs in from any device. Local models can run on your own hardware when policy calls for it.

Can We See It on Our Own Work?

Yes. Book a pilot and bring one real workflow.
