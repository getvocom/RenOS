Credit Questions

What Is a RenOS Credit?

One credit is one cent of model usage, so 1 credit = $0.01. A task that costs 31 cents at the provider uses 31 credits.

Do You Mark up Model Usage?

No. Credits are spent at each model's published rate. When a provider cuts prices, your cost drops the same day.

Who Supplies the Credits?

Credits are supplied through our carrier partner Vocom. You can also bring your own keys on Enterprise, or run local models at zero credit cost.

Do Credits Expire?

Pro's included credits pool across your team each month. Purchased credits stay valid for 12 months.
