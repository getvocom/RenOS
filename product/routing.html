Frequently Asked Questions

Can We Really Use Any Model?

You can use any model your admin approves. Policy decides which one each task may use.

Can People Pick a Model by Hand?

Yes. Auto is the default, and a manual choice is a governed override that's logged like everything else.

When Are Rules Enforced?

At routing time, before any model is called.

Why Do Different AI Models Matter?

Models differ in strength and cost. Routing lets you match each task to the right one without asking people to choose.
