Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Audit Trail Automatic?

Yes. Every task writes an entry with no extra step for the person doing the work.

How Long Are Audit Logs Kept?

Pro keeps 90 days. Enterprise keeps one year or more, with export.

Can We Use AI for Audit Work Itself?

Yes. Audit teams use RenOS like any team, and their own tasks are logged the same way.
