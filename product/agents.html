Frequently Asked Questions

What Is an AI Agent in RenOS?

An agent is an AI worker that does or watches something in your other systems, through connectors, under an approval tier and a hard cap.

Can AI Agents Work Together?

Yes. A crew runs several agents with named roles on one objective, as one governed session, and every handoff is recorded.

Who Checks the Agents' Work?

Reviewers never grade their own work. An independent verifier checks rules, and Tier-3 actions always wait for a human.

Can an Agent Run up a Big Bill?

No. Agent tasks and crews always carry a hard ceiling, and the run stops when it reaches it.
