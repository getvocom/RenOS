Frequently Asked Questions

What Can Builder Mode Make?

A finished thing: a document, a small app, a report or a workflow, delivered as an artifact in your project.

What Does It Cost?

Medium: the plan plus one charge per unit built. You see the estimate on the plan before anything runs.

Can Builder Compare Models?

Yes. Split view runs up to four approved models side by side so you can keep the best result.

Is the Rejected Output Kept?

The record shows every version and which one was accepted.
