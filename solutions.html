What a Rule Looks Like

Same Engine, Different Vocabulary

Healthcare

WhenSource is the patient-records drive

ThenAllow approved or local models only. Log every override.

Property Management

WhenDraft is a rent-increase notice

ThenRequire manager approval before sending.

Credit Union

WhenTopic is lending policy

ThenAnswer only from the current policy document, and cite the section.

Examples are illustrative. Packs are starting points you edit and simulate before publishing.
