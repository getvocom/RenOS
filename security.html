What We Do

Controls You Can Point To

Tamper-Evident Audit Trail

Each record is chained to the one before it. Any change breaks the chain and shows up on verification. See the audit trail.

Identity and Access

Single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, roles and per-project permissions.

Data Handling

Data residency options, configurable retention, and export or deletion on request.

Local and Offline Models

Keep confidential work on your own hardware. Policy can require it for chosen data.

Keys Never in Prompts

Provider keys and connector secrets are stored encrypted and never placed in model context.

Least-Authority Actions

Connectors get the minimum scope, and every action carries an approval tier.
